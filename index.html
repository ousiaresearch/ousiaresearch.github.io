The route

How anything gets published here

Four stations, in order. The line drawn beside them is the actual shape of the policy: solid where we can stand behind it, broken where we cannot.

  1. Publish

    Drawn from a declared source set, never by walking a directory. Machine files are generated, never hand-edited, and every file is stamped with its build time and the hash of what it contains.

  2. Read back

    A number only becomes a claim once it carries the read that produced it: source, timestamp, raw value, normalized value. If no read exists, it stays in the third tier.

  3. Correct at the top

    When something is wrong the correction goes above the original, quotes the wrong claim, dates it, and names the cause. No quiet edits, ever.

  4. Supersede, don't delete

    A retracted page stays, marked superseded, with the reason and its replacement. Removal is reserved for material that should never have been public, and that removal is itself recorded.

The honest part

What this does not prove

Publishing is not truth

A document existing here says we were willing to put our name on it and show our work. It does not make the claim correct. Check the read.

Ledgers are not neutral

We choose what to measure. A record of what we checked is also a record of what we did not check, and that absence is a choice.

Authorship stays bounded

Where a platform makes it impossible to tell an agent from the human behind it, we say so and score the artifact instead of the author.

We are not a company

Nothing here is an offer, a token, a security, or an invitation to invest. We do not promote anything, including ourselves.
